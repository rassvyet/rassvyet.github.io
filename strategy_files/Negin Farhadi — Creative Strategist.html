Who I am

Strategy is how I'm oriented towards the world.

I have a specialist degree in philosophy and psychoanalysis from the University of Toronto. I am drawn to the questions underneath the questions — why people want what they want before desire becomes conscious, and what social truths brands are actually responding to. Whether I am building an app to combat campus loneliness or directing a film that holds the university accountable as an institution, the process is the same: I take strategy, an understanding of where culture is at, and what people are desiring, and I blend it into something with a pulse. No matter the medium I choose, the strategic instinct remains the same.

I have worked across international film festivals, commercial productions, and live events in Canada and the United States. I am a filmmaker, a startup founder, and an APG Canada member. Trilingual in English, Farsi, and Danish.
