Who I am

## Strategy is how I'm oriented toward the world.

I have a specialist degree in **philosophy and psychoanalysis** from the University of Toronto. I am drawn to the questions underneath the questions — why people want what they want before desire becomes conscious, and what social truths brands are actually responding to through their creative work.

I have worked across international film festivals, commercial productions, and live events in Canada and the United States. I am a filmmaker, a startup founder, and an **APG Canada member**. Trilingual in English, Farsi, and Danish.
