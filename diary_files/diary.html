August 23

One of those days where I feel that I am losing my spark. I've been trying to re-engage with my hobbies to stimulate some whimsy and wonder back in me. But it's strange, none of this was brought about by any bad news. It's a spontaneous bout of saddness that obviously chooses to attack me the day before a very important interview. I feel that maybe revisiting some of my worst memories (in effort to try and help A with his problems), I have brought it up way too close to the surface. People do not want their bubbles popped. They need their copes, prickles and all, to keep the brisk breeze from getting to them again.

I have made so many promises - ambitions one, nonetheless - to myself about what I will do. But this summer was odd. It flew by much too fast, And i was abnormally happy with letting it skim past. Yet, at the same time, I made conscious efforts to plan every other day or weekend with something. Why do I feel like time was not on my side, still? Why do i feel gone with the wind?

I will brush myself off. At least I fixed my website up and decided to add something new to it. Even if it is totally ripped off from Fairygore. But reading her website makes me so happy. She has no idea what a muse she is to me. She reminds me of myself as a little girl. Funny to think that we live in proximity to each other. But I dont want to disrupt that fantasy, better to pretend that she'll like me, and keep the phantasy protected in my own mind. As she says, she feels a spiritual ennui with her writing. Me too. And her response was to keep a virtual diary log. I will try my best to maintain mine as well, let's see....

Last words I have are just that I am hoping after tomorrow, I will have a little break to get myself back on track. I just want to be done with this interview. I would love to get the job, it would be a fantastic conclusion to a devastating few months without a job. But honestly, if they cannot see worthy in my words and my demeanor, I don't care. I need to continue my path. My true path.

Some website thoughts i had were: creating an interactive train station or house, or perhaps a journey through some rooms, or maybe even a cross-continental travel! Oh, there could be a ghibli time travelling machine, and it brings you to various places in my life and world. It could be ithica circa 948, it could be my old childhood bedroom, it could be a most wonderful scene from a film I love. I could add and add to it. and give people options of going whereever they like. It sounds like monstrous planning, but it'll be fun? I told A that I wished it could be winter again, that I could be sitting in my room, blinds open, with view of the snowwy street, the luminous moon reflected in each striking snowflake. And i would be playing minecraft in the dead of night, not a single person to bother me, no work to wake up to. Just me, in a world i can escape to. I would like it very much to be winter, even if for a brief moment. Why am i getting nostalgia all the time for past places and spaces? I do wish I'll have a vivid dream soon, so i can revisit some people I can no longer see. Right now, im feeling better than i have in the last 24 hours. Symptoms of anxiety are coming back, and I don't know why. I'm just trying to push through it, push push and push myself to some better happiness. I have to cook soon, but I am not in the mood. I wonder what I can do to maintain the energy. But now, i need to rehearse for this interview. I must try. Adeiu.

August 24-27

Oops! The goal of consistent writing has taken a preemptive nosedive, leaving me crestfallen and blue. But fret not, I'll remedy my lapse and come back stronger! Right... so what happened the last few days? On monday after my interview, I took Dzhal out for his birthday. Fed him the most juiciest kabobeh koobideh, and he was so content with the food (while it was still uneaten on his plate) that he had an expression of exhaustion on his face, like someone who is already regretting experiencing something so finite, knowing the euphoric sensation is not an eternal state, basking in the nausiatic happiness. I don't know how else to put it. I hope i conveyed it well. Anyways, I was so happy to watch my friend be happy. He ate, we talked. Afterwards we went to a nearby cafe for some sweets and tea, and upon climbing the narrow stairs to the second floor (where the waitress told us was the coffee-floor), we saw that there was a long room with several seats, and at the far end of the room, the wall was lined with windows... and... and a door! a door that led to a balcony! We approached it with disbelief, not knowing that the back of this plaza would be so thoughtful as to include a balcony overlooking the dropoff-loading area. With the 50 inch width that they had (it was a narrow balcony) they had kindly put some tables, seats, and a flower bed hanging off the railing. But something else caught our eye, why yes it did. a fucking ASHTRAY. we found an ASHTRAY. delighted and overwhelmed with glee, dzhal looking at me and i looking at him, we both found something treasured in our home countries. the ability to have a fag in the balcony of an establishment. it is banned in canada, so this was like finding king tut's back molar or something. we promptly sat outside, enjoyed out tea, coffee, cake, cigarettes, conversation, and photoshoot. it was amazing.

My grandma is leaving soon and I'm feeling mixed. On the one hand, i allocated a lot of my time to her this summer, hanging out and being able to thoroughly enjoy her serene affect. She is a true angel, both in composure and in ideology. I admire her and adore her. Revere her, in fact. I would love to visit her in Denmark soon, since i miss my country a lot.

Besides that, I was feeling a little lazy the last few days but to my suprise, even if i tried to waste my own time now, I've established such discipline that I still manage productivity. I am honestly proud of myself. I wish i could say more things here but unfortunately I must keep my most private thoughts for paper. I cannot risk anyone finding it.

I look forward to Fall actually. It is my favourite month. I do think however i need to take advantage of the hot weather. I really wanted this summer to be more going out, more seeing Lia. But things don't always work out the way you want them to. You have to be smart and adapt before your opportunities are whisked away.

I love the full moon, I'm going to plan something nice for it.

I would like to throw a caroline party.
